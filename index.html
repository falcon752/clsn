About Us

Candlelight Foundation for Children
with Special Needs

Candlelight Foundation for Children with Special Needs is a dedicated non-profit organization committed to making a meaningful difference in the lives of children with exceptionalities and their families.

We understand the unique challenges these children face and recognize the importance of supporting their overall well-being in a caring, inclusive environment.

Discover More
Our Mission

Supporting Children and Families

Our mission is to support children with special needs and their families by providing access to education, therapy, emotional care, and meaningful community engagement.

Our Vision

Believing in Every Child

We envision a world where every child is recognized for their potential, supported with dignity, and given the opportunity to live a fulfilling and meaningful life.

Community Engagement

Building Awareness and Inclusion

By promoting inclusivity and awareness, we work to build a supportive community where children with special needs and their families feel understood, connected, and empowered.

About

Legal Status & Registration

Candlelight Foundation for Children With Special Needs, Inc. is a United States–registered nonprofit organization recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN: 33-2403724).

In addition to its U.S. registration, the Foundation is also formally registered in Nigeria and operates programs, services, and community initiatives supporting children with Autism, ADHD, Dyslexia, and other developmental needs across both regions.

Our commitment
Our dual registration allows us to responsibly serve families, collaborate with partners, and receive donations in compliance with applicable laws in each country where we operate.
