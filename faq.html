FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Search our FAQs to find answers about therapies, programs, support resources, and enrollment.

General

We offer a range of specialized therapies including behavioral therapy, speech therapy, and cognitive therapy. These therapies are designed to help children with Autism, ADHD, and speech dyslexia improve their social skills, communication, and cognitive functions.

Our specialized educational program includes individualized learning plans tailored to each child's unique needs and abilities. We focus on developing both academic and life skills, using adaptive learning materials and techniques.

We provide a range of supportive resources and services, including assistive technology, social and emotional support, and physical and occupational therapy. These resources are designed to support the overall development of children with Autism, ADHD, and speech dyslexia.

Yes, we do offer in-home services. Our team of professionals can provide therapies, educational programs, and supportive resources in the comfort of your home. This allows us to create a familiar and comfortable environment for the child, which can enhance the effectiveness of our services.

We offer comprehensive training programs for parents and caregivers. This includes workshops on understanding specific exceptionalities like Autism, ADHD, and Dyslexia, strategies for effective communication with children with special needs, guidance on creating a supportive home environment, and emotional and psychological support.

You can contact us directly through our website or by phone to discuss enrollment. Our team will guide you through the process, which includes an initial assessment to understand your child's unique needs and how we can best support them.

We cater to children of all ages, from babies and toddlers to adolescents. The exact age range can vary depending on the specific program or service. Please contact us directly for more information.

Our team of professionals conducts a thorough assessment of each child to understand their strengths, areas of difficulty, and personal interests. Based on this assessment, we develop an individualized plan that includes specialized therapies, an educational program, and supportive resources tailored to the child's unique needs.

Yes, we offer some services remotely to ensure we can support children and their families regardless of their location or circumstances. This includes online therapy sessions, virtual parent and caregiver training, and digital resources. Please contact us for more information about our online services.

The safety and well-being of the children in our care is our top priority. We have stringent policies and procedures in place to ensure a safe and nurturing environment. This includes thorough staff training, regular safety audits, and a strong focus on respect and inclusivity.

We welcome support from individuals and organizations in various forms, including donations, volunteering, and partnerships. Please visit our website or contact us directly to learn more about how you can support our work.

Yes, all our services including specialized therapies, educational programs, supportive resources, and parent and caregiver training are offered at no cost, thanks to our generous donors and sponsors. We believe that every child deserves access to the support they need, regardless of their financial situation.
